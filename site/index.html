Restocking for small shops in Lagos

Order on WhatsApp. Pay the supplier after the rider checks.

Tell StockRun what your shop needs. It finds wholesalers near you, haggles in your name and books a bonded rider. The supplier is paid when the rider has counted the goods at pickup. A circuit on X Layer makes that call, not the AI.

How an order runs

Six steps from a text message to stock on your shelf

The AI does the talking. Money only moves at the two handovers, and only when a circuit says so.

  1. Step 1

    You send the order

    Plain English or Pidgin: "20 cartons of Indomie, 10 bags of rice, within 5 km." StockRun reads it against a product list and confirms the quantities.

  2. Step 2

    It finds suppliers

    Wholesalers within your radius who stock the item. Your preferred suppliers come first, then approved ones. Blocked suppliers are never contacted.

  3. Step 3

    It haggles on WhatsApp

    Starts 5% under the ask, meets halfway, and walks away above your ceiling. Every message says the supplier is paid once the rider has checked the goods.

  4. Step 4

    Money is locked

    The vault asks RestockGuard. Inside your budget and limit, the goods and rider fee are held in escrow. Otherwise it asks you, or refuses.

  5. Step 5

    Rider checks at pickup

    A rider with a posted bond counts the goods at the supplier. PickupCheck pays the supplier for what was counted, in naira.

  6. Step 6

    Delivered to your shop

    DeliveryCheck pays the rider when you confirm. If cartons are missing, you are paid back from the rider's bond.

Two handovers, two checks

The rules are wired in logic gates, so nobody can talk them round

Each rule is a TapeOut circuit built from NAND gates. The vault gathers the inputs from its own storage and signed evidence, then reads the circuit's answer. The circuit can only answer. It cannot send money or change anything.

RestockGuard

119 NAND · 34 in → 5 out

Runs when the agent proposes an order.

  • REJECTSupplier unknown or blocked
  • REJECTOver the remaining weekly budget
  • ASK OWNERAbove your auto-approve limit
  • ASK OWNERAlcohol, tobacco or high-value goods
  • ASK OWNERSupplier more than 10 km away
  • APPROVEEverything else: money goes into escrow

PickupCheck

210 NAND · 27 in → 13 out

Runs when the rider reports from the supplier.

  • HOLDRider is not at the supplier
  • HOLDRider has no bond posted
  • HOLDDamaged goods reported
  • PAY PARTFewer counted than ordered: pay for the lower of the photo count and the rider's count
  • PAY FULLBoth counts match the order

DeliveryCheck

141 NAND · 21 in → 13 out

Runs when the goods reach your shop.

  • WAITRider is not at the shop, or damage reported
  • COMPENSATEShort delivery: you are paid from the rider's bond
  • WAITYou raised an issue
  • PAY RIDERYou confirmed, or the confirmation window ran out

The AI never moves money

The agent's key can propose an order and nothing else. It cannot pick where money goes, approve its own order or submit pickup evidence.

No deposits to suppliers

The supplier is paid at pickup, after the count, in naira through an off-ramp partner. There is no "send half first".

A failed check holds the order

If a circuit call fails, nothing is paid. The default is to wait, never to pay.

Your yes has limits

Approving an order clears "ask owner" rules. It never overrides a reject, so a blocked supplier stays blocked.

Who it is for

Three people, one order, nobody trusting a stranger

Shop owners

  • Restock without closing the shop to go to market
  • Set a weekly budget and an auto-approve limit
  • Pay only for what was counted and delivered
  • Short delivery is paid back from the rider's bond

Suppliers

  • Orders arrive on WhatsApp, as they do today
  • Paid in naira at pickup, once the count is done
  • No chasing payment after the goods leave

Riders

  • Post a bond once to take higher-value jobs
  • Fee set up front: ₦1,500 plus ₦250 per km
  • Paid automatically when the owner confirms or the window runs out
