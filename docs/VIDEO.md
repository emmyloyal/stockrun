# Demo video: script and shot list (under 3 minutes)

Record on a phone screen (or a narrow browser window) at https://stockrun.onrender.com. Open the site a minute before
recording so the free server is awake. Use Paystack **test mode**. Have two accounts: a buyer (any email) and the
admin (emmyloyal0@gmail.com, in a second browser).

**Before recording:** buyer signed in with naira deposited (Paystack test card); a seller with an approved bank account
(or record that step live); location allowed in the browser. Read lines in your own words.

| Time | Screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Title card, then Mile 12 photo or the StockRun home | "Buying stock in Lagos means half a day at the market, or paying up front and hoping it all arrives. StockRun is an AI buying agent where your money only moves when you have the goods, and the decision is made on-chain." |
| 0:15–0:40 | Ask tab: type "20 cartons of Indomie", budget ₦300,000, area "Ketu", tap **Find sellers** | "I say what I want, my budget and my area. One word is enough. The AI works out what kind of seller sells this: for a bulk order it looks for wholesalers first, and finds the closest real businesses with a WhatsApp number." |
| 0:40–1:00 | Tap **Message on WhatsApp**, show the AI-written message, tap **Open WhatsApp chat** | "It writes my first message, without ever revealing my budget, and opens my own WhatsApp chat with the seller. StockRun never messages anyone for me." |
| 1:00–1:30 | **Record the deal**: item, 4 cartons, ₦14,000, Food staples → **Record deal and lock the money**; show the ✓ message | "When we agree a price, I record the deal. This is where the chain comes in: RestockGuard, a circuit we taped out on X Layer, checks the seller, my budget, the kind of goods and the distance, and only then is my money locked." |
| 1:30–1:45 | Orders tab: the order **Locked**, open the circuit trail (circuit ID, block, input, output) | "Every decision is saved with the circuit, the X Layer block and the exact input and output, so anyone can ask the circuit again and check it." |
| 1:45–2:15 | At (or simulating) the shop: **I have the goods** → count 3 → **Yes, I have them: approve payment** | "At the shop, with the goods in my hands, I confirm what I counted. My phone must be at the shop. PickupCheck approves the seller's payment for three cartons only; the fourth carton's money comes straight back to me." |
| 2:15–2:35 | Admin tab (second browser): payout **Paid via Paystack**, "Sellers buyers contacted" | "The seller is paid to their bank through Paystack, automatically, exactly what the circuit approved. The admin can watch and hold a payout, but can never create one or change an amount." |
| 2:35–2:50 | README "What is on-chain" table, or the three tapeout transactions on OKLink | "Three circuits, taped out on X Layer mainnet. The AI finds and writes; it never moves money. Circuits decide every payment." |
| 2:50–3:00 | StockRun logo + link | "StockRun. Your money moves when your goods do." |

**Keep it honest on camera:** say "test mode" when Paystack shows; don't call naira balances on-chain; if you show the
location step from home, say you are simulating being at the shop (or record it at a real shop).

**Fallback if something breaks while recording:** the demo agent (Ask tab → "Try the demo agent") runs the full flow
with bot sellers and the same circuits.
